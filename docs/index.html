What do Momentum and Range mean?
Momentum score (0–100)
A price-momentum read on how aligned and strong the recent move is — not a signal derived from options flow or fundamentals. Three components, weighted:
  • Trend alignment (40%) — how many of the 1D / 1W / 1M changes agree on direction. All three moving the same way scores higher than a choppy mix.
  • Magnitude (30%) — the average size of those moves. A flat, directionless instrument scores low here regardless of alignment.
  • Relative strength (30%) — the 1M return versus the instrument's own benchmark (^HSI for HK names, ^STI for SGX names). Beating the market counts for more than a move that's just the whole market drifting.
High momentum means the recent move is consistent, sizeable, and outperforming its market — not that the instrument is a good buy. A stock can have high momentum on the way down.
Range score (0–100)
Where the price sits in its own trailing 90-day range. 100 = at the 90-day low, maximum room before hitting recent resistance. 0 = at the 90-day high, little room left before likely mean-reversion. This is a range-position proxy only — it is not the GEX-based feasibility model used in Basket Command Centre 2 (options-derived gamma walls, call/put walls, max pain). That model needs an options chain; HK/SGX GEX coverage was checked and is not currently available — most SGX REITs have no listed options market at all. Treat this score as "room to move," not "probability of reaching a target."
Analyst price target & upside
Sourced from Yahoo Finance's own aggregation of sell-side analyst estimates (targetMeanPrice, high/low range, analyst count, consensus recommendation) — not calculated by this dashboard. ETFs and preference shares structurally don't get analyst price targets and will show no analyst coverage; that's expected, not a data gap. A separate fetch failed status means Yahoo's data call itself failed on the last run (a known intermittent issue) — that's a network hiccup to retry, not a real absence of coverage. Where coverage exists, analyst counts can run as low as 3–4 — check the count before weighting the target.
